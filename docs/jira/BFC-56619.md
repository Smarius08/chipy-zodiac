## Animation \[TBD\]

General rules, as suggested in BFC-56106 \[TBD – confirm in Figma\]:

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Hands | “Keep the existing gesture, subject to checking its playback performance.” |
| Zodiac illustration | “Keep it stationary. Animate one highlight over it.” |
| Particles | “Optional, sparse and brief. Remove them first if performance suffers.” |
| Crystal ball | “Keep its position and size fixed. Use one subtle rim pulse.” |
| Result | “Fade in the text, then stop decorative movement.” |
| Reduced motion | “Use a simple fade with static artwork.” |

Per sign, as suggested in BFC-56106 \[TBD – confirm in Figma\]:

| Sign | Animation | Detail to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aries | A copper highlight travels along both horn curves, releasing a few upward sparks. | Keep the ram fully visible; avoid a large flame effect. |
| Taurus | Light traces the horns, then passes gently through the surrounding leaves. | The bull stays still; movement comes from light and a slight leaf sway. |
| Gemini | Each profile lights in turn. Two fine ribbons meet between them, producing one small spark. | Both profiles remain visible throughout. |
| Cancer | A silver highlight crosses the crescent while one soft ripple expands beneath the crab. | Keep the crab stationary and separate from the ball. |
| Leo | A warm highlight moves outward through the mane, briefly forming a subdued solar halo. | Avoid a bright burst that competes with the result. |
| Virgo | Wheat tips sway slightly as a champagne highlight travels along the stems. | Keep the maiden’s face and body still. |
| Libra | The pans make one small, symmetrical sway and settle level. A highlight crosses the beam. | Don’t tilt the scales according to the score. |
| Scorpio | A burgundy highlight follows the segmented tail to its tip, then fades. | Keep the whole scorpion visible; avoid a striking or lunging motion. |
| Sagittarius | Light traces the bow and arrow, followed by a short streak toward a nearby star. | Leave the complete bow and arrow in place after the effect. |
| Capricorn | Small lights climb the surrounding mountain path and finish at the horn tips. | Keep the sea-goat and fish tail clearly visible. |
| Aquarius | A short stream flows from the vessel, separates into light particles and fades near the ball’s rim. | Keep the stream outside the content area. |
| Pisces | A light trail follows the curve around the two fish; their fins move gently once. | Avoid a full orbit that takes either fish behind the ball. |

## Error States

Errors appear when the user clicks **Reveal My Luck** and a field is invalid. They look the same as in BFC-52416. Each invalid field shows:

* an error border on the input
* an error icon
* the error message directly below the input

If both fields are invalid, both errors are shown at the same time, each below its own field. They must not overlap. Use the positions in Figma.

## Final Result Layout

As in BFC-52416, there is **one result structure** shared by all four Final Luck Categories. Display:

* **Final Luck Score** with `/10`
* **Final Luck Category**
* **Daily Luck Reading Message**
* **Reset** CTA
* **Zodiac**, **Lucky Day(s)** and **Lucky Numbers** (info strip)
* Tool disclaimer

#### Result Category Colours

Each Final Luck Category keeps its colour and assets from BFC-52416 on every sign page: Jackpot (teal/cyan), Good (blue), Some (purple/magenta), Low (red/burgundy). Use the exact colours and assets in Figma.

The category colour must stay clearly visible against each sign’s theme. Where they are close (Low Luck on Scorpio; Jackpot on Sagittarius and Aquarius; Good Luck on Aquarius), follow the adjusted treatment in Figma: \[TBD – Figma\]

## Daily Luck Reading Message

The message below the Final Luck Category varies in length. The message pool is in BFC-52417. Test the layout with the longest message, on desktop, tablet (768px and 600px) and mobile.

The full message must:

* be fully visible, with no text cut off
* not overlap the Reset CTA or other result content
* stay inside the crystal ball, clear of the sign artwork
