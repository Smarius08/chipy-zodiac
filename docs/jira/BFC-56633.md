Layout of the two sections on each sign page that sit between the in-page nav and the Lower Section & FAQs. Behaviour: [**BFC-56885**](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56885). Data: [**BFC-56621**](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56621).

**Design:** [Figma – Zodiac Lucky Tool](https://www.figma.com/design/mRGsMU76MmiKMnzQkwYKgG/Tools---Calculators?node-id=14455-33331). The file has three versions (V1, V2, V3), drawn for Aries and Taurus. **Build V1:** desktop frame "Desktop" (Aries) and "Mobile – 360px width" (Aries), with Taurus as the second colour example. The filled V1 badge is the only version whose fill can change by level. If design picks V2 or V3 instead, update this ticket.

### Both sections

* Light layout on the page background (#F9FAFA). There is no dark panel and no glow; this replaces the dark-panel direction in BFC-56106.
* Show only level names, dates, weekdays, day counts and the supporting message. Never show scores, totals or "/10".
* Level names: **Quiet, Mixed, Strong, Strongest**. Use the same names in the badge, the calendar and the legend.
* All mock data in Figma is placeholder (dates, cards, cell levels, counts). Real values come from BFC-56621, as described in BFC-56885.

### 1. Lucky Strength Level

In-page nav label: "Today's Outlook". H2: per sign, from BFC-56617 (e.g. Aries: "Is Today a Lucky Day for Aries to Gamble?"). The heading "Today's Outlook" in Figma is a placeholder.

**Desktop**, top to bottom:

1. **Badge** (left): a pill with the level as a word (Quiet, Mixed, Strong or Strongest). The pill fill is that level's colour from the table above. Which level: BFC-56885.
2. **Text** (right of the badge):

    * Line 1: "\[Sign\] Day Outlook" (bold), then "for \[Weekday\], \[Month\] \[D\], \[YYYY\]", e.g. "Aries Day Outlook for Wednesday, November 4, 2026".
    * Line 2: the supporting message for the sign and level, from the table in BFC-56885. It replaces the placeholder text in Figma ("Typical outlook for Aries. Your personal score…").
    
3. **Cards label:** "Next Strongest Days for \[Sign\] in \[Year\]" (uppercase in Figma). Don't use "Next 5": there can be fewer than 5 cards.
4. **Date cards:** a row of up to 5 equal cards. Each shows the date ("November 16") and, under it, the weekday ("Monday"). Background: the sign's Quiet colour. Display-only: no hover, no link. Which dates: BFC-56885.

**States** (when each applies: BFC-56885):

* 5 cards: the row fills the width, as in Figma.
* 1–4 cards: the cards keep the same width and align left; they don't stretch. \[TBD – Figma\]
* No cards: the row is replaced by the one-line note "No Strongest dates remain this year." \[TBD – Figma\]
* Badge in all four levels (Figma only shows Strongest): \[TBD – Figma\]

**Mobile:** the badge sits on top, then the text, then the cards label, then the cards in a 2-column grid (5 cards = rows of 2, 2, 1).

**Longest text:** check "Sagittarius Day Outlook for Wednesday, September 30, 2026", "NEXT STRONGEST DAYS FOR SAGITTARIUS IN 2026" and the longest supporting message in BFC-56885, on desktop and mobile.

### 2. Current-year Calendar

In-page nav label: "\[Year\] Calendar". H2: per sign, from BFC-56617 (e.g. Aries: "Aries Lucky Days to Gamble in \[Year\]: Month by Month"). The heading "2026 Calendar" in Figma is a placeholder.

**Desktop:**

* A grid with 12 rows (Jan–Dec, short month names on the left) and one cell per day (up to 31 per row).
* Dates that don't exist (e.g. 30 February, 31 April) have no cell; the row is shorter.
* Each cell is filled with the colour of its level. Cell size in Figma: about 24 × 22px, rounded corners.
* Today's cell has a dark outline (#140A1C in Figma). Which cell: BFC-56885.
* **Legend** under the grid, in one row: the four level swatches, each with "\[Level\] - \[N\] days", then the outlined square with "Today". Counts: BFC-56885.

**Mobile:**

* BFC-56885 requires one month at a time, with previous/next arrows and a row of month chips, opening on the current month. The current mobile frame shows the whole year with cells about 6px wide, which are too small to tap. The month view is needed in Figma: \[TBD – Figma\].
* Cells must be large enough to tap (at least 44 × 44px including spacing).
* The legend sits under the month, wrapping onto 2–3 lines as in Figma.

**Date details (all viewports):** a cell's date and level, e.g. "Nov 10, 2026 · Strongest". Shown on hover or tap: BFC-56885. Look and position: \[TBD – Figma\].
