### Definitions

For every sign _s_ and valid calendar date _d_ = (day, month, year):

* W(s): the sign’s lucky weekday indices, Monday = 0 through Sunday = 6. K(s): the sign’s original Zodiac Lucky Numbers (Reference Data, below).
* digitSum(n) = sum of the base-10 digits of n
* reduce9(n) = 1 + ((n − 1) mod 9), for a positive integer n
* **Date Number Total** T(d) = digitSum(day) + digitSum(month) + digitSum(year). Leading zeros don’t affect it. Example: 03/11/2026 gives 15.
* **Date Numerology Number** R(d) = reduce9(T(d)). Example: 15 gives 6.
* K_reduced(s) = {reduce9(k) : k in K(s)}. A set: duplicate reduced values count once.
* weekday(d) is calculated from the full Gregorian date; it changes with the year.

### Lucky Day Score

* distance(a, b) = min(|a − b|, 7 − |a − b|) (circular: Sunday and Monday are one day apart)
* D(s, d) = min of distance(weekday(d), w) over w in W(s) (the nearest lucky weekday when a sign has two; scores are not added)
* **Lucky Day Score = 3 − D(s, d)**: exact weekday 3, one day away 2, two days away 1, three days away 0

### Lucky Number Score

* E(s, d) = \[T(d) in K(s)\] OR \[R(d) in K_reduced(s)\]
* A(s, d) = min |R(d) − k| over k in K_reduced(s), on the straight 1–9 scale (1 and 9 are not neighbours)

| First matching condition | Lucky Number Score |
| --- | --- |
| E(s, d) is true | 2 |
| E(s, d) is false and A(s, d) = 1 | 1 |
| Otherwise | 0 |

Multiple matches don’t stack.

### Total and level

**Sign-and-date total S(s, d) = Lucky Day Score + Lucky Number Score** (an integer from 0 to 5).

| Quiet | Mixed | Strong | Strongest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–1 | 2 | 3–4 | 5 |

Strongest requires both an exact lucky weekday and an exact number match. All operations are integer operations. No annual ranking, tie-breakers, averaging, rounding, percentiles or forced distribution apply. Equal totals receive equal levels, regardless of date or sign. Different signs may have different numbers of dates in each level.

### Data for the front end (per sign)

* The level of every date of the displayed year (365 dates, or 366 in a leap year). Nonexistent dates (e.g. 31 April) have no record.
* The number of dates per level across the whole year; the four counts total 365 or 366.

Available at server render, for [BFC-56885](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56885). Send **dates, level names and day counts** only; the component scores and totals never reach the browser.

### Caching and year change

* Data can be reused per sign and year; the caching and delivery mechanism is an implementation choice.
* Leap years: a year divisible by 400, or divisible by 4 but not by 100. Use that year’s actual weekdays and date digits.
