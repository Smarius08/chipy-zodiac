# BFC-56885 — Zodiac Lucky Day to Gamble: Lucky Strength Level & Calendar Functionality

Source: https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56885
Retrieved: 2026-11-09
Jira updated: 2026-11-05T14:25:59.865+0200
Snapshot of description only; refresh before implementation if live source is available.

Behaviour of the two sign sections below the tool: Lucky Strength Level (badge, supporting message, upcoming Strongest dates) and the Current-year Calendar (cells and legend). Layout: [**BFC-56633**](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56633). Data: [**BFC-56621**](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56621) (adopted formula v1.0).

### Both sections

* In the HTML on page load (server-rendered).
* “Today” is the visitor’s local calendar date. How the local date is obtained and applied is the developers’ choice. Today’s level and the upcoming Strongest dates come from the year’s levels in [BFC-56621](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56621).
* Use only the per-date levels from BFC-56621. No client-side scoring, ranking, tie-breaking or averaging.
* **Consistency:** the badge and the calendar must show the same level for the same sign and date.
* When the visitor’s local date changes, select the new badge date, upcoming dates and today outline. On 1 January of the visitor’s local date, both sections switch to the next year’s levels and legend counts.

### 1. Lucky Strength Level

* **Badge:** shows the level of the page’s sign for the visitor’s local date (Quiet, Mixed, Strong or Strongest).
* **Supporting message:** the approved message for the page’s sign + that level, from the table below. A fixed lookup: no random selection and no personal inputs. Separate from the personal tool’s Daily Luck Reading Messages. Not shown in calendar cells.
* **Upcoming Strongest dates (cards):** Strongest dates of the page’s sign **strictly after** today (today is excluded), within the current year, in chronological order, at most 5. Each shows its date and weekday. Don’t fill the list with Strong dates or next year’s dates. Chronological order selects the nearest future dates; it isn’t a luck tie-breaker.
* The cards are display-only, with no click action.
* **States:**

    * 5 or more remaining → show the next 5.
    * 1–4 remaining → show only those.
    * None left → show “No Strongest dates remain this year.”
    

**Examples (Aries, 2026):** on 5 Nov the cards show 15 Dec (Tuesday) and 22 Dec (Tuesday), so 2 dates; on 30 Dec none are left; on 1 Jan the cards show 6 Jan, 24 Mar, 31 Mar, 14 Apr, 21 Apr.

### 2. Current-year Calendar

* Each date of the current year shows its level from BFC-56621; today’s cell is marked. Nonexistent dates (e.g. 31 April) have no cell level.
* **Legend:** the number of dates in each level across the **whole** displayed year, including past dates (not only the visible month or the remaining dates). The four counts total 365, or 366 in a leap year.
* **Date details:** on devices that support hover, hovering a cell shows its date and level; on touch devices, tapping a cell shows its date and level. Both use the same date and level. Presentation: [BFC-56633](https://gtmarketing.atlassian.net/browse/BFC-56633).
* **Mobile:**

    * One month at a time; opens on the visitor’s current local month.
    * The previous/next arrows and the month chips change the month shown. Month navigation changes the visible dates only; it doesn’t recalculate their levels.

### QA

* The badge and the calendar agree for every sign and date.
* Upcoming dates are strictly after today, in chronological order; check the 5, fewer-than-5 and none-left states. Cards have no click action.
* Legend counts sum to 365 or 366.
* The supporting message matches the sign + level lookup exactly, and is the same on every visit for the same sign and level.
* Date details: hover on hover-capable devices, tap on touch devices; both show the same date and level.
* The mobile calendar opens on the visitor’s current local month.
* Changing a visitor’s name or date of birth doesn’t change any sign-section result.
